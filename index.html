Our impact

We built Arul Health after listening to thousands of patients and caregivers. The same gap came up every time: plenty of information, and no one to handle the logistics.

  • 3,000+

    Patients and caregivers interviewed before we wrote a line of code

  • 80%

    Of a frontline navigator's time spent on logistics instead of counseling

  • 10–15%

    Of eligible oncology patients who ever reach the support programs available to them

  • 5–7×

    Times a patient typically needs to hear about a service before it registers

Figures from Arul Health's patient and caregiver research, 2024–2026.

Our focus

To unlock healthcare's full potential to improve lives, we focus on three key areas: Access to Care, Healthy Habits, and Stronger Communities.

  • Access to Care

    Handling the appointments, financial aid, records, rides, and paperwork that stand between a diagnosis and the care people are entitled to

    Learn more

  • Healthy Habits

    Meeting people in the messages they already use so that follow-through on medications, visits, and everyday health becomes routine

    Learn more

  • Stronger Communities

    Giving nonprofits, clinics, and health systems the platform at no cost, and pairing patients with navigators who share their lived experience

    Learn more
