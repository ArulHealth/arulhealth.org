The program

A grant of technology, people and connections

Most grants give an organization money and leave it to find the tools. Arul Reach works the other way round.

We grant the organizations that know their communities best the things we are good at building and running. Members receive the platform, the team behind it, and introductions to the people who fund patient support. Arul Reach is selective, and it is free for every organization we accept.

  • 01Technology

    Your own AI care navigation platform, under your name, with usage credits covered by Arul.

  • 02Technical support

    A deployment team that sets up and runs your program, so your staff does not have to.

  • 03Funding opportunities

    Introductions to the sponsors, donors, research institutions and public agencies that fund patient support.

  • 04Resources and data

    The latest Arul tools and resources as we release them, and a dashboard of your community's data for grants and board reports.

01 · What members get

Everything a navigation program needs, granted

Your own navigation platform
AI care navigation under your brand, by iMessage, SMS, WhatsApp or phone. It applies for financial aid, collects records, books visits, tracks screening schedules and explains bills. Peer navigators step in when a patient needs a person.
Your own data dashboard
De-identified, aggregate data on your community: engagement, access gaps, adherence, financial toxicity and geographic barriers. Use it in grants and board reports.
Access to the Arul network
Introductions to the sponsors, donors, research institutions and public agencies that fund patient support.
A deployment team on the ground
Arul Reach sets up and runs your program, so your staff does not have to.
No cost
Nothing for your organization or your patients.
02 · Who it is for

Organizations that share the mission

Arul Reach is for nonprofits, community-based organizations and patient groups that serve patients and caregivers directly. We accept a limited number of members so that each one gets a full deployment team.

We look for three things.

  1. Health equityYour work reaches people the system serves least: by income, geography, language, age or diagnosis.
  2. Access to careYou help people get the care, aid and support they are entitled to, not only information about it.
  3. Shared principlesPatients come first, their data is protected, and the people you serve are never charged.

03 · A data commons for good

Data is seen only by vetted organizations and trusted partners driving research and new possibilities

Navigation produces something most patient organizations have never had: a clear picture of where their community gets stuck. Members own that picture. Shared data is always de-identified and aggregated.

04 · Common questions

What organizations ask us first

Our community has specific needs. How long does setup take?

About 3 to 6 months. Setup adds your conditions, resources, languages and brand to a platform that already serves many conditions, from cancer to diabetes.

How does Arul stay accurate for our patients?

We build with your staff and advisors, use the guidance and resources they approve, and update as research and programs change.

What data do we get?

A dashboard of de-identified, aggregate data: engagement, access gaps, adherence, financial toxicity and geographic barriers. New datasets on your community for grants and research.

What does it cost?

Nothing, for your organization or your patients. The platform carries your brand.
